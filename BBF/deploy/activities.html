Activities & Projects

What BBF does — fellowship, service, development, sport, welfare, and faith. All activities rooted in the BBF Constitution, Articles 19 & 20.

Constitution, Articles 19 & 20

Faith in Action.
Brotherhood in Motion.

BBF is not just a membership — it is a fellowship that moves. The Constitution authorises BBF to organise a wide range of activities that bring members together, develop their abilities, and channel the brigade spirit into positive impact for God and society.

All activities must promote positive relationships, responsible conduct, and the values of BBF. Any member can propose an activity — it simply needs to go through the appropriate approval channel.

Our Programmes

Eight Areas of Activity

BBF channels its energy into eight key programme areas — each serving members, communities, and the cause of brotherhood.

Constitution, Article 20

Community Service

BBF shall encourage members to demonstrate the principle of service through meaningful contributions to the following — as specified in the BBF Constitution, Article 20.

"Members shall be encouraged to use their talents, time and resources positively for the benefit of society."
— BBF Constitution, Article 20
The Church

Supporting church activities, outreach, and the broader work of the Christian community through BBF member participation.

The Boys' Brigade

Contributing positively to The Boys' Brigade — volunteering as officers, supporting Brigade activities, and promoting the movement.

Local Communities

Clean-up exercises, neighbourhood support, distribution of relief items, and tangible contributions to community well-being.

Schools

Mentorship visits, book donations, career talks, and educational support to pupils and students in local schools.

Charitable & Development Causes

Support for legitimate charitable causes and community-development initiatives that align with BBF values and objectives.
