BBF Chapters

BBF brings together Boys' Brigade members across companies, churches, councils, and locations — find your chapter or help start one.

Constitution, Article 5.3

What is a BBF Chapter?

A BBF chapter is a local gathering of Boys' Brigade members connected through the Fellowship. BBF may bring together members from:

Churches

Members from different churches and denominations — united by their shared Brigade identity and Christian faith.

Companies

Active members from various Boys' Brigade companies who wish to maintain fellowship beyond their company activities.

Councils & Dioceses

Members from different Boys' Brigade councils, dioceses, and organisational structures across a given area.

Locations

Members from the same town, city, or area — regardless of which company, church, or council they belong to.

Important — Art. 6

BBF chapters shall not replace or interfere with the authority and activities of any Boys' Brigade company, church, council, diocese, or other recognised Brigade structure. Membership of a BBF chapter does not exempt any member from responsibilities to their respective Brigade company or church.

Constitution, Article 5.3

The Scope of BBF Fellowship

BBF promotes unity across all these dimensions — without interfering with the legitimate structures and responsibilities of individual members.

Different Companies

Members from different Boys' Brigade companies come together as one fellowship within BBF.

Different Churches

BBF is interdenominational — members from Baptist, Anglican, Methodist, RCCG, CAC, and all churches are welcome.

Different Councils

Brigade councils and dioceses may differ, but the shared identity of Boys' Brigade membership unites all.

Different Locations

Members from different states, cities, towns, and communities — one fellowship regardless of geography.

Active & Former Members

Both currently active Boys' Brigade members and former members who remain committed to Brigade values are welcome.

Brigade School Certified

New members must hold the Brigade School Certificate. Existing members without it may remain during the transition period.

Grow the Fellowship

Start a Chapter
in Your Area

If there is no BBF chapter in your area and you are a Boys' Brigade member who shares our values, you can take the initiative to bring brothers together. Starting a chapter begins with registering as a BBF member and contacting the BBF Secretary.

Every chapter must operate in line with the BBF Constitution and shall at all times respect the authority of The Boys' Brigade in its area.

Register as a Member Contact the Secretary
Step-by-Step Process
1
Register as a BBF Member

Complete your BBF membership registration. You must be an active or former Boys' Brigade member and hold (or be working towards) the Brigade School Certificate.

2
Identify Interested Members

Reach out to fellow Boys' Brigade members in your area — from your company, church, council, or location — who share the values and purpose of BBF.

3
Contact the BBF Secretary

Write to the BBF Secretary expressing your intention to form a chapter, providing details of the proposed location and the members who would be involved.

4
Await Approval & Guidance

The BBF Executive will review your application and provide guidance on the formal recognition process for the new chapter.

5
Hold Your Inaugural Meeting

Once approved, hold your inaugural chapter meeting, elect chapter officers, and begin your fellowship activities in line with the BBF Constitution.
