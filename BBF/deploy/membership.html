Join the Fellowship

BBF is open to all active and former Boys' Brigade members. One Brigade. One Brotherhood. One Purpose.

Who Can Join

Membership Eligibility

BBF membership is open to any person who is an active or former member of The Boys' Brigade and meets the conditions set out in the BBF Constitution, Article 7.

BBF Constitution, Article 7
Membership is open to active and former Boys' Brigade members who hold the Brigade School Certificate, agree to abide by the Constitution, and are registered through the approved process.
Why Join BBF

What Membership Gives You

As a BBF member you gain more than a membership card — you gain a brotherhood.
