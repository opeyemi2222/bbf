Who We Are

A Fellowship Built on
Brigade Brotherhood

Brigade Brotherhood Fellowship (BBF) is a fellowship platform for members of The Boys' Brigade — built on Faith, Fellowship, Service, and Friendship.

BBF brings together current and former Boys' Brigade members — from different companies, churches, councils, and locations — to build genuine relationships, support one another, and demonstrate the Brigade spirit through service to God, the Church, and society. BBF respects and upholds the authority, values, and discipline of The Boys' Brigade at all times. We are not a replacement — we are the fellowship that keeps the brotherhood alive.

Our Purpose

To provide a platform through which Boys' Brigade members can build genuine relationships, promote unity, support one another, and participate in positive fellowship and service. — BBF Constitution, Art. 4.1

Our Foundation

Christ is our foundation. BBF operates on the basis of Christian faith and encourages all members to demonstrate Christian character in their conduct and relationships. — BBF Constitution, Art. 2
