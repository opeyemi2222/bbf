BBF Executive Officers

Elected officers serving the fellowship in accordance with the BBF Constitution, Articles 10, 11, and 12.

Constitution, Article 11.1

Coordinator / President

The Coordinator/President provides overall leadership, presides over meetings, and ensures the BBF Constitution is respected at all times.

Constitution, Article 10

The Executive Committee

All nine offices of the BBF Executive as established by the Constitution — each with defined duties under Article 11.

Constitution, Article 10.1

General Responsibilities of Leadership

All executive officers of BBF — regardless of role — share these ten collective responsibilities.

Constitution, Article 12

Elections & Tenure of Office

How BBF executive officers are elected, how long they serve, and the requirements for candidacy — as established by the Constitution.

Election Process Art. 12.1

Executive officers shall be elected by eligible members through a process approved by the Fellowship. Elections are held at a properly constituted general meeting of BBF members.

Eligibility for Office Art. 12.2

Be a recognised member of BBF
Demonstrate commitment to the Fellowship's objectives
Have a record of responsible conduct
Be willing and available to perform the duties of the office
Satisfy any additional requirements approved by the Fellowship

Tenure & Vacancy Art. 12.3 – 12.5

Tenure: two (2) years, unless amended
Officers may be eligible for re-election for another term
Vacancies: Executive may appoint an eligible member to act temporarily
Removal requires due process and an opportunity for the officer to respond
Serve the Brotherhood

Interested in a Leadership Role?

BBF leadership positions are filled through an election process approved by the membership. Any recognised BBF member who meets the eligibility criteria in Article 12.2 and is willing and available to serve may put themselves forward. Contact the BBF Secretary for information on the current election process.

To Stand for Office — Art. 12.2

Be a recognised member of BBF
Demonstrate commitment to the objectives of the Fellowship
Have a record of responsible conduct
Be willing and available to perform the duties of the office
Contact the Secretary
