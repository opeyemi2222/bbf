Constitution, Article 19

BBF may organise fellowship gatherings, social events, community service, educational programmes, skills development, sports, music, welfare initiatives, networking activities, Christian programmes, and Brigade-support activities.

Organise with BBF

Want to Organise an Event?

BBF members and chapters can propose fellowship activities, community service days, skills sessions, or social events. All activities must be consistent with the objectives of BBF and approved through the appropriate channel.

Contact the BBF Social/Event Coordinator or the Secretary to discuss your proposal. Chapter-level events should go through your chapter coordinator.

What BBF Can Organise — Art. 19

Fellowship gatherings
Social events and recreational activities
Community service activities
Educational and skills development programmes
Sports and recreational activities
Music and talent development programmes
Welfare and networking initiatives
Christian and Brigade-support programmes
All activities must promote positive relationships, responsible conduct, and the values of BBF. — BBF Constitution, Art. 19
