About Brigade Brotherhood Fellowship

Our identity, purpose, Christian foundation, guiding values, and the Brigade bond that connects every member.

Who We Are

A Fellowship Platform for
Boys' Brigade Members

Brigade Brotherhood Fellowship (BBF) is a fellowship platform established for members of The Boys' Brigade — built to promote Christian faith, brotherhood, friendship, fellowship, service, unity, discipline, personal development, team spirit, and positive social interaction.

BBF recognises that members may belong to different companies, churches, councils, dioceses, organisations, and locations. Nevertheless, the shared Brigade identity provides a foundation upon which meaningful relationships and cooperation can be built. BBF serves as the platform through which members can come together, support one another, develop their abilities, share experiences, serve God and society, and strengthen the bonds of friendship and brotherhood.

BBF shall at all times respect the authority, structure, regulations, objectives, and discipline of The Boys' Brigade. BBF is not a replacement for The Boys' Brigade — and shall never operate as a competing structure.

Purpose, Aims & Objectives

Why BBF exists — and what it seeks to accomplish for every member and community.

01

Our Purpose

To provide a platform through which members of The Boys' Brigade can build genuine relationships, promote unity, support one another, and participate in positive fellowship and service.
— BBF Constitution, Art. 4.1

02

Our Christian Foundation

BBF shall operate on the foundation of Christian faith and shall encourage members to demonstrate Christian character in their conduct and relationships. The Boys' Brigade shall remain the common Brigade foundation and identity connecting all members of BBF.
— BBF Constitution, Art. 2

03

Our Relationship with The Boys' Brigade

BBF shall not be a replacement for, or a competing structure to, The Boys' Brigade. BBF shall recognise and respect the existing authority, leadership, structure, regulations, and discipline of The Boys' Brigade at all times.
— BBF Constitution, Art. 6

Article 4.2
